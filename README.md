# Alessandro Ferreira Paz Lima

Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at **Universidade Santa Cecília (UNISANTA)**, Santos, Brazil,
advised by Prof. João Inácio da Silva Filho. I work on **Paraconsistent Annotated Logic (PAL2v / LPA2v)**
applied to robotics, automation and sensor fusion: how to keep a system working and making sound decisions
when its sources of information contradict each other.

*Mestrando em Engenharia Mecânica (UNISANTA). Pesquisa: Lógica Paraconsistente Anotada aplicada à robótica e à automação.*

## Research interests
- Paraconsistent Annotated Logic with two-valued annotation (PAL2v): Para-Analyzer, PAN, ParaExtrctr, paraconsistent neural cells
- Fault-tolerant fusion of redundant sensors for mobile robots and drones
- Paraconsistent control (PAL2v-PI) and decision-making under contradictory evidence
- ROS 2, Gazebo and PX4 SITL simulation; embedded systems (Arduino, ESP32)
- Industrial automation and dimensional quality inspection
