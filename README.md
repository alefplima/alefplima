# Alessandro Ferreira Paz Lima

Founder and director of **[Keysed](https://keysed.com.br)** (Digital Educational System) · Higher-education professor of
Computing · 15 years of technical and programming experience.

I am a master's student in Mechanical Engineering (Control and Automation) at **Universidade Santa Cecília (UNISANTA)**,
advised by Prof. Dr. João Inácio da Silva Filho. My research is on **Paraconsistent Annotated Logic (PAL2v / LPA2v)**:
keeping robots, controllers and automation systems working, and making sound decisions, when their sources of
information contradict each other.

## Research
- **Dissertation (UNISANTA, 2025 – ):** *Paraconsistent Annotated Logic (LPA2v) abstracted into No-Code programming for
  microcontroller control and automation*
- PAL2v algorithms: Para-Analyzer, Paraconsistent Analysis Node (PAN), ParaExtrctr, paraconsistent artificial neural cells
- Fault-tolerant fusion of redundant sensors for mobile robots and drones; paraconsistent control (PAL2v-PI)
- ROS 2, Gazebo and PX4 SITL simulation; Arduino/ESP32 and IEC 61131-3 PLCs
- Computer vision and expert systems; industrial quality inspection
